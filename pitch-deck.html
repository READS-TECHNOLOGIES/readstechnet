05Architecture

Two chains, each carrying the work it's actually good at.

$READS rewards run on Stellar. $READS certificates and academic records live on Cardano. The split is deliberate, not incidental.

Stellar
Rewards & payments
  • Distributes every learner reward and handles in-platform token payments.
  • Sub-cent fees and settlement in seconds, built for high-volume, small-value transfers.
  • No per-transaction minimum-balance requirement standing between a student and their reward.
Cardano
Certificates & records
  • Anchors blockchain-issued certificates and academic credentials permanently.
  • Chosen for durability and verifiability — a record meant to last, not to move often.
  • Every $READS credential remains independently checkable on-chain, worldwide.

Cardano requires locking at least 2 ADA in every transaction output — a real cost when you're paying out thousands of small rewards as a bootstrapped startup. Stellar removes that overhead entirely for rewards, while Cardano stays exactly where it's strongest: permanent, tamper-proof records.
